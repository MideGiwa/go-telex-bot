Our mission is to connect people and foster seamless communication, enabling teams to achieve their goals more efficiently. We believe in providing a secure, reliable, and user-friendly environment for all your communication needs.

## VPN Policy

For secure access to internal resources, all employees are required to use the company's Virtual Private Network (VPN).

**How to connect:**
1. Open the VPN client application.
2. Enter your company credentials.
3. Select the "Primary Network" profile.
4. Click "Connect".

If you encounter any issues, please contact IT support.
